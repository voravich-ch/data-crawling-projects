# Rate Your Music — README

The website provides meta-data on the recording music market and interactions
developing among participants to the online community of enthusiasts.
Particularly there four domains of information that are relevant for my research:

1. product-level meta-data (e.g., credits, genres, descriptors)
2. product reviews and ratings
3. the categorization of products (i.e., participants voting 
   in favor or against the application of a certain genre to a 
   certain music album)
4. the creation and maintenance of the set of categories one can associate with
   products (i.e., participants voting in favor or against the creation
   of a certain genre)
