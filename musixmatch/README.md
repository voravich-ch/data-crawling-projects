# Musixmatch ― README

Bologna-based established new venture; they claim to have the world's largest
repository of lyrics. Key figures: unique lyrics 14 M; the community is ~ 6 M;
lyrics added (contributions, I guess) ~ 40 M.

The website presents some interesting elements to analyze lyrics from a research
perspective:

+ [research paper on the importance of vocals in the mind of listeners](http://ismir2018.ircam.fr/doc/pdfs/98_Paper.pdf)
+ [music emotion detecton](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.04831)

There are also simple [examples](https://developer.musixmatch.com/showcase) showing how to use lyrics data:

+ Genre with the largest vocabulary ― A comparison of vocabulary sizes of popular music genres
+ Predicting the Grammys with data ― Ever wondered which popular musician is the most potty-mouth...
+ The Largest Vocabulary in Music ― An analysis of vocabularies of top selling musicians
+ Hip hop lyrics are the most profane ― Followed by Heavy Metal while Folk & Country lyrics are the least profane
+ Adele’s new album: It’s all about the lyrics ― A study of search trends and user engagement
+ Are Adele’s Hello and Tom Waits’ Martha similar? ― Over the last couple of weeks Adele’s new single “Hello” has …
+ Johnny Cash has been everywhere (Man!) ― A hack by Iain Mullan using Musixmatch api and Johnny Cash
+ Lyrics Ipsum ― Generate Lorem Ipsum from your favourite band’s lyrics
+ Most profane artists in music ― Ever wondered which popular musician is the most potty-mouthed…
